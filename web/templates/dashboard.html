How these deals are selected

We show up to 12 upcoming itineraries, using the latest observed price for each one. Each price is compared with all valid USD observations for its route in the last 90 days, including that latest price and past travel dates. Provider estimates and configured baselines are not used here. Delivery age settings do not filter this list. Prices may have changed since observation.
